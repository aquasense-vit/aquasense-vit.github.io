EDI Project 1 CS-C · Group 10 VIT Pune · 2026–27

Water only when the soil asks for it.

AquaSense reads soil moisture with an ESP32, switches a pump on its own when the soil gets dry, and stops it when the soil is wet enough. Every drop goes where the roots need it, and you can watch and control it all from your phone.

    Your plants

    Every plant, its own pump

    Each plant waters itself automatically. Switch any plant to manual to take over its pump. In manual mode you get an alert if a plant drops below its low limit with its pump off, or goes above its high limit with its pump on. The limits are 5% and 95% unless you change them in Settings.
